❓FAQ

Which OnePlus phones does Tropsi-X-Kernel support?

OnePlus 8, OnePlus 8 Pro, OnePlus 8T and OnePlus 9R.

Which ROMs and Android versions work?

Stock OxygenOS 13.1 (A13) and OxygenOS 14 (A14), and custom ROMs on A13 and A14. Newer custom ROMs and port ROMs are not supported yet.

What is Tropsi Flasher?

An Android flashing utility by TropsPk. It flashes kernel and recovery ZIPs and partition images, and can back up and restore the kernel partition. Root is required.

Which root options does Tropsi-X-Kernel include?

BakaSU, SUSFS, NoMount and Baseband Guard, with KernelSU Official, KOWSU and BakaSU managers.

How can I support Tropsi-X-Kernel?
